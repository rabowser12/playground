Frequently Asked Questions

What can I expect during my first session?

Your first session is an opportunity for us to get to know each other. We'll talk about what brings you to therapy, your goals, and your history. There's no pressure — it's simply a conversation to help us understand how I can best support you.

How long are sessions, and how often will we meet?

Sessions are typically 50 minutes long. Most clients begin by meeting weekly, though the frequency can be adjusted based on your needs, schedule, and progress over time.

Do you accept insurance?

We are currently in-network with [Insurance Provider A] and [Insurance Provider B]. We also offer out-of-network billing support and a sliding scale fee for clients who qualify. Please contact us to discuss your specific situation.

Is therapy confidential?

Yes. Everything discussed in therapy is strictly confidential. There are a small number of legally mandated exceptions — such as if there is imminent risk of harm to yourself or others — which we will review together in our first session.

Do you offer telehealth / virtual sessions?

Yes, we offer secure HIPAA-compliant telehealth sessions for clients located in [State]. Virtual sessions are available on the same schedule as in-person appointments.

How do I know if therapy is right for me?

If you're feeling stuck, overwhelmed, or simply want a dedicated space to work through something, therapy can help. We offer a free 15-minute consultation call so you can ask questions and see if we're a good fit before committing to a full session.
